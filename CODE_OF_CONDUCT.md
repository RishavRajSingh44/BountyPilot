# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome regardless of experience, identity or background. Be respectful, constructive and patient. Critique ideas and code, provide useful reproduction steps, credit others and respect boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, credential disclosure and persistent personal attacks are unacceptable. Do not publish private contact details or data. This policy applies in issues, pull requests and other official project spaces.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, warn participants, limit participation or ban repeated/severe abuse. Responses should be proportionate, protect affected people and allow a fair explanation where safe. Maintainers follow the same standards.

Report public misconduct through GitHub's comment-reporting tools. For confidential reports, contact [RishavRajSingh44](https://github.com/RishavRajSingh44) through a private channel you already share; otherwise request a private channel without incident details. A dedicated confidential reporting address has not yet been established. Vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
